Cameron earned his B.S. in Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering from the University of Connecticut in 2024, where he investigated fluidized bubbling bed reactor dynamics applied to chemical-looping combustion systems and high-fidelity cyber-physical modeling under the guidance of Prof. Burcu Baykal. He joined the lab in 2025 and is co-advised by Prof. Chris Kieslich and [Prof. Fani Boukouvala (GT-ChBE)](https://ddpse.github.io). His research focuses on developing surrogate models for anisotropic nanoparticle interactions and dynamics with extensions to multi-phase flow hydrodynamics using sparse sampling techniques.
